Our Heritage

Founded in 1976, Shahi Mithai began as a small shop in the heart of the city. Today, we are proud to be the most trusted name in premium Indian confectionery.

Our Head Halwai, Master Chef Ram Prakash, brings 40 years of expertise, ensuring every piece of sweet carries the authentic taste of tradition.
